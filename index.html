About

Over 20 years I have worked as a test engineer, developer, project manager and software architect, from tactical control systems and national statistics platforms to delivery, pricing and privacy products.

Java is my core language; I also work with Scala and Python. Most of my recent work is around Kafka, Kafka Streams, Akka Streams, Spark and Elasticsearch on Kubernetes.

These days I guide development teams, design software architectures and define development standards.
